## The profile follows where the text goes

With profiles per application, a dictation now takes its profile from the application in front when the recording **ends**, not when it starts. Without Accessibility, that is the app whose service the second press of the shortcut calls.

- When a dictation goes into another application later, with **Paste Last Transcript** (⌃⇧V on macOS) or the macOS service, DanyWhere looks at that application's profile and redoes only what differs: it transcribes the recording again, post-processes it again, or only takes that profile's **Append** text.
- An entry you changed in History stays as you wrote it; only the **Append** text follows the application.
- The window whose earlier dictations go along as context is the one in front when the recording ends.

## The overlay on the screen you work on

On macOS and Windows the overlay shows on the screen of the window in front, dialogs included, else on the screen with the pointer, with or without Accessibility. Before, without Accessibility it always showed on the main screen. On Linux it still follows the pointer.

## Also new

- **Copy Last Transcript** (⌃⇧C on macOS, Alt+Shift+C elsewhere) copies the dictation profile's **Append** text too.
- The star in History says what it does: **Keep recording permanently**. Such a recording is never deleted, even when the storage for recordings is full.
